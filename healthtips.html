Heath Tips.

Consuming healthy foods, beverages, and snacks, and getting regular physical activity may help you reach and maintain a healthy body weight. Making suitable lifestyle choices may also help men and women prevent some health problems.

Tips:


  • Eat More Whole Foods

    • Focus on fruits, vegetables, whole grains, nuts, lentils, and lean proteins. These keep you full longer and reduce overeating.


  • Control Portion Sizes

    • Even healthy foods can add up if portions are too large. Use smaller plates or measure your servings when possible.


  • Stay Hydrated

    • Drink 2–3 liters of water per day. Many people mistake thirst for hunger.


  • Reduce Sugary Drinks

    • Avoid sodas, packaged juices, and energy drinks. They add calories without making you full.


  • Prioritize Protein in Every Meal

    • Protein boosts metabolism and helps muscle building:
    Eggs
    Chicken
    Paneer
    Lentils
    Greek yogurt


  • Improve Sleep Quality

    • Aim for 7–9 hours. Lack of sleep increases hunger hormones and cravings.


  • Move Your Body Daily

    • You don’t need gym membership. Try:
    30–45 mins brisk walking
    Home workouts
    Skipping
    Cycling


  • Track Your Eating

    • Not forever—just for a few days. It helps you spot patterns like unnecessary snacking.


  • Avoid Highly Processed Foods

    • Chips, instant noodles, fast food, etc. They usually contain high sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.


  • Be Consistent, Not Perfect

    • Small habits done daily beat strict diets. Aim for long-term changes, not quick fixes.

Workout / Diet plan For Weight Loss

Workout Plan:

  • DAY 1

    • 30–40 min brisk walk or light jog
    • 3×15 bodyweight squats
    • 3×20 jumping jacks

  • DAY 2

    • 10-min warm up (dynamic stretches)
    • 3×12 lunges (each leg)
    • 3×12 push-ups (knee push-ups OK)
    • 10–15 min steady-state cycling or walking

  • DAY 3

    • 20–30 min interval cardio (walk/jog intervals)
    • 3×20 mountain climbers
    • 2×45–60 sec plank

  • DAY 4

    • 35–45 min brisk walk / light hike
    • 3×15 glute bridges
    • 3×12 slow burpees (or half-burpees)

  • DAY 5

    • 20–25 min HIIT (beginner): 30s on / 30s off ×10–12 rounds
    • 3×30 sec side planks (each side)
    • Mobility & stretching 10 min

  • DAY 6

    • 40–50 min low-intensity cardio (walk/cycle)
    • 3×15 squats
    • 3×15 bent-over rows (use bottles if no weights)

  • DAY 7

    • Active recovery: light yoga or long walk 30–45 min
    • Full-body mobility + stretching session

Diet Plan:

  • DAY 1

    Breakfast: Oats with milk/water, berries or banana, handful of nuts.
    Lunch: 2 rotis + dal + mixed vegetable sabzi + salad.
    Snack: Apple or Greek yogurt.
    Dinner: Grilled chicken or paneer + large salad (light dressing).

  • DAY 2

    Breakfast: Poha or upma with veggies.
    Lunch: Brown rice (small) + rajma/beans + cucumber salad.
    Snack: Green tea + a small handful of roasted peanuts.
    Dinner: Clear vegetable soup + boiled eggs or sprouted moong salad.

  • DAY 3

    Breakfast: 2 boiled eggs or paneer bhurji + a slice of whole wheat toast.
    Lunch: 2 rotis + grilled/fried fish or chicken curry (light) + salad.
    Snack: Fruit bowl (seasonal fruits).
    Dinner: Khichdi (light) + mixed salad.

  • DAY 4

    Breakfast: Smoothie (spinach + banana + protein milk/yogurt).
    Lunch: Rice (small) + dal + stir-fried vegetables.
    Snack: Roasted makhana or carrot sticks with hummus.
    Dinner: Vegetable stir-fry with tofu/paneer (small portion) + salad.

  • DAY 5

    Breakfast: Idli (2–3) + sambar + chutney.
    Lunch: 2 rotis + spinach sabzi + dal + cucumber.
    Snack: Buttermilk or a small handful of almonds.
    Dinner: Grilled fish/chicken or paneer wrap (whole wheat, lots of veggies).

  • DAY 6

    Breakfast: Dalia (broken wheat) with vegetables and a few dry fruits.
    Lunch: Vegetable pulao (small) + raita.
    Snack: 1 fruit or low-fat yogurt.
    Dinner: Tomato soup + vegetable omelette or sprout salad.

  • DAY 7

    Breakfast: Vegetable dosa or 2 small parathas (light) + curd.
    Lunch: 2 rotis + chicken/fish or paneer + salad.
    Snack: Trail mix (small portion) or fruit.
    Dinner: Light dinner — soup + salad or a bowl of khichdi.
